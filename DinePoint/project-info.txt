DinePoint

Mobile Application

DESCRIPTION:

Midpoint Feature

PROBLEM:
Finding a restaurant is relatively easy when everyone is in the same area, but choosing a convenient meeting point becomes difficult when people are coming from different parts of the city.

DinePoint was designed to address this problem by helping groups find restaurants around a common geographical midpoint.

SOLUTION:
DinePoint allows users to explore restaurants, filter them based on different preferences, save favourites, view restaurant details and find restaurants near a calculated midpoint.

Users can select locations for the people in their group, calculate a midpoint and then explore suggested restaurants around that location.

KEY FEATURES:
• Restaurant discovery
• Search and filtering
• Cuisine, rating, price, ambience and area filters
• Restaurant details
• Favourite restaurants
• Map visualisation
• Midpoint calculation
• Restaurants near the calculated midpoint
• Google Maps navigation
• Sharing midpoint restaurant recommendations

MAIN FEATURE:
Midpoint-based restaurant discovery.

DinePoint calculates a geographical midpoint between selected locations and uses that point to identify nearby restaurant options.
